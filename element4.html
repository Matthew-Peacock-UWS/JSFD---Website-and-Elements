About the Scene

Element 04

Start at the main menu, turn the sound on and press Play. Inside the walled compound, collect all eight medkits and ammo cans while zombies shamble after you. Every pickup updates the counter, and every zombie hit drains the health bar and flashes the screen red.

Beyond the lab

  • An animated 3D graveyard behind the menu
  • How to Play panel and a pause menu with Resume, Restart and Main Menu
  • Win and game-over panels - the survivor dances when you win
  • Zombies chase you when you're close, wander otherwise and groan nearby
  • The GUI is scaled with idealHeight so it fits any screen size

Assets

  • HVGirl.glb character: Babylon.js asset library (assets.babylonjs.com)
  • All textures, skybox, music and sound effects: original, made for this project
