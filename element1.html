About the Scene

Element 01

An abandoned quarantine checkpoint on the edge of town. A guard booth with a spinning warning beacon watches over a road barrier that keeps raising and lowering, an infected sample hovers above a table, and a zombie paces up and down behind the quarantine fence.

Beyond the lab

  • Night-time atmosphere with exponential fog, a glow layer and a flickering street lamp
  • Keyframe animation (Animation class) on the barrier arm, with pauses built into the key frames
  • A spot light parented to a rotating node so the beacon sweeps red light across the scene
  • A zombie built from primitive meshes that walks between two waypoints

Assets

  • Concrete, crate, rusty metal, metal plate and hazard-stripe textures: original, made for this project

What This Scene Demonstrates

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓
    ShadowsShadowGenerator on the moonlight with percentage-closer filtering
  • ✓
    MotionKeyframed barrier, spinning beacon, floating sample and a walking zombie
