About the Scene

Element 02

A small town abandoned to the dead, sitting in a valley of height-map hills under a moonlit sky. Boarded-up houses line the road up to a ruined church, with a graveyard, wrecked cars and a bonfire in the square. The camera slowly circles the town until you take control.

Beyond the lab

  • Houses and the church are built from several meshes merged into one, keeping each part's material
  • Instances used for 60 dead trees and the gravestones so the scene stays fast
  • A bonfire made from two particle systems (fire and smoke) with a flickering light
  • Zombies that wander the streets and follow the height of the terrain
  • The camera auto-rotates when idle

Assets

  • Ground, planks, roof tiles, concrete, bark and rust textures: original, made for this project
  • Night skybox (6 images) and height map: original, made for this project
  • Wind and music audio: original, made for this project

What This Scene Demonstrates

  • ✓
    Mesh manipulationHouses, church, trees and gravestones merged with Mesh.MergeMeshes, then cloned and instanced
  • ✓
    Height-map terrainCreateGroundFromHeightMap with a tiled texture; objects placed with getHeightAtCoordinates
  • ✓
    360° backgroundNight skybox (CubeTexture) with the moon painted in
  • ✓
    LightingMoonlight with shadows, flickering street lamps, firelight and fog
  • ✓
    Constrained cameraArcRotateCamera with radius and angle limits and panning switched off
  • ✓
    AudioWind and music loops, switched on with the SOUND button
