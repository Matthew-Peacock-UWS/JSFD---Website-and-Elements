About the Scene

Element 03

A walled supply yard at night. Walk the survivor around, push the supply crates into the green safe zone, topple the crate pyramid and knock over the zombie training dummies. Crates in the zone turn green and the counter shows how many have been secured.

Beyond the lab

  • Third-person camera that swings in behind the player while walking
  • An animated capsule physics body so the player can push dynamic objects
  • Zombie training dummies built as a model riding on a physics box
  • The crate counter and safe-zone light react to what happens in the scene
  • Hold Shift to run - the walk animation speeds up to match

Assets

  • HVGirl.glb character: Babylon.js asset library (assets.babylonjs.com)
  • Concrete, brick, crate, rust and hazard-stripe textures: original, made for this project
