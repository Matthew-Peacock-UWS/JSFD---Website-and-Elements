About the Scene

Element 05

The town has fallen. In the Outskirts, find the keycard beside the burning car and reach the bunker door in the hills. Inside the Bunker, make your way through dark corridors and side rooms to the radio and call for rescue. Your health carries over from one level to the next.

Beyond the lab

  • Four scenes on one page: menu, the Outskirts, the Bunker and the Rescue
  • Door and radio triggers using ActionManager intersection events
  • Shared game state (health, keycard, time) carried between scenes
  • An indoor level with flickering lights, a rotating siren and camera collisions
  • A rescue scene with a hovering helicopter and searchlight

Assets

  • HVGirl.glb character: Babylon.js asset library (assets.babylonjs.com)
  • All textures, skybox, height map, music and sound effects: original, made for this project
The full breakdown of these scenes, with code extracts, is on the Documentation page.
